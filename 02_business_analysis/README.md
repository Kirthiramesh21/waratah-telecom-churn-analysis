# Phase 2 — Business Analysis

Translates the Phase 1 objectives into testable requirements and governed metric
definitions.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| [BRD_business_requirements.md](./BRD_business_requirements.md) | What the business needs and why — objectives, MoSCoW requirements, key questions, assumptions, constraints, risks, traceability |
| [FRD_functional_requirements.md](./FRD_functional_requirements.md) | What must be built — data requirements, data quality rules, functional and non-functional requirements |
| [kpi_definitions.md](./kpi_definitions.md) | Authoritative formula, grain, inclusions and traps for every metric |

**BRD vs FRD:** the BRD is owned by the business and answers *what problem are we
solving*. The FRD is owned by delivery and answers *what must the system do*. If a
requirement names a table, a chart or a tool, it belongs in the FRD.
